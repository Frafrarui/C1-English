Inversions

Master advanced sentence structures

Interactive Theory

Never, Rarely, Seldom, Little

Inversion in the main clause.

  • Never Never have I lifted such a heavy weight on the bench press.
  • Rarely Rarely do you see someone with such perfect execution.
  • Seldom Seldom does he miss a training session.
  • Little Little did I know that eating those extra calories would make such a difference.

Hardly / Barely / Scarcely

Followed by "when".

Past Perfect + when + Past Simple
  • Example Hardly had I finished my sets when the gym closed.

No sooner

Followed by "than".

Past Perfect + than + Past Simple
  • Example No sooner had I drank the protein shake than it was time for the next meal.

Under no..., In no..., On no..., At no..., Not only...

Inversion in the main clause.

  • Under no circumstances Under no circumstances should you sacrifice technique to lift more weight.
  • In no way In no way will I accept that kind of behavior.
  • On no account On no account must this door be left unlocked.
  • At no time At no time did my partner doubt my ability to pass the Advanced exam.
  • Not only Not only did he arrive late, but he also forgot his notes.

Inversión en la Segunda Parte (Main Clause)

Aquí tienes las principales expresiones con las que la inversión se va a la segunda parte:

Not until (No hasta que...)
Not until + [oración normal] + [INVERSIÓN]
  • Example Not until he apologized did I forgive him.
Not since (No desde que...)
Not since + [oración normal] + [INVERSIÓN]
  • Example Not since I was a child have I eaten so much candy.
Only when (Solo cuando...)
Only when + [oración normal] + [INVERSIÓN]
  • Example Only when you finish your homework can you play.
Only if (Solo si...)
Only if + [oración normal] + [INVERSIÓN]
  • Example Only if it rains will we cancel the picnic.
Only after (Solo después de...)
Only after + [oración/tiempo] + [INVERSIÓN]
  • Example Only after the meeting ended did she realize the mistake.

So + adj/adv

Usado para enfatizar una cualidad o manera.

Original

She was so supportive that I managed to study every single day.

Inversion

So supportive was she that I managed to study every single day.

Such + (a/an) + noun

Usado para enfatizar un nombre o nombre con adjetivo.

Original

It was such a rigorous routine that I needed extra rest.

Inversion

Such was the rigor of the routine that I needed extra rest.

1st Conditional (Should)

Should + subject + bare inf, ... will/can/may/must + bare inf.
  • Example Should you need more rice for lunch, let me know and I'll cook it.

2nd Conditional (Were)

Were + subject + to + inf, ... would/could/might + bare inf.
  • Example Were I to fail the test, I would simply prepare and take it again.

3rd Conditional (Had)

Had + subject + past participle, ... would/could/might + have + past part.
  • Example Had we known you were coming, we would have organized a better dinner.

Speaking Strategy (C1/C2)

Estructuras comodín casi siempre aplicables en el examen (con traducción).

Dónde usarlo: Part 3 (Collaborative Task). Cuando estéis evaluando las diferentes opciones y quieras apoyar una de ellas dando dos grandes ventajas de golpe.

Not only + [inversión]..., but + [sujeto] + also...
  • Example Not only does this approach save time, but it also provides a much better outcome in the long run.
  • Traducción Este enfoque no solo ahorra tiempo, sino que también ofrece un resultado mucho mejor a largo plazo.

Dónde usarlo: Part 4 (Discussion). Cuando el examinador te pregunte sobre cómo ha cambiado la vida moderna, el impacto de la tecnología o el trabajo en equipo.

Had it not been for [noun phrase], [consecuencia]...
  • Example Had it not been for the recent advancements in technology, dealing with this problem would have been nearly impossible.
  • Traducción Si no hubiera sido por los recientes avances tecnológicos, lidiar con este problema habría sido casi imposible.

Dónde usarlo: Part 4 (Discussion). Cuando habléis de problemas globales (medio ambiente, sedentarismo, falta de comunicación cara a cara).

Rarely do we see...
  • Example Rarely do we see people taking this issue as seriously as they should nowadays.
  • Traducción Rara vez vemos a la gente tomarse este problema tan en serio como deberían hoy en día.

Comparisons

Master your comparative structures

Grammar Theory

Syllable rules

1 syllable (-er), 2 syllables ending in -y (-ier), 2+ syllables (more + adj).

  • 1 syllable Fast → Faster (He is faster than me.)
  • Ends in -y Heavy → Heavier (This box is heavier.)
  • 2+ syllables Expensive → More expensive (It is more expensive.)

Intensifiers for MORE

far, much, a lot, a great deal, significantly, twice, easily, by a long way.

  • Far This method is far better than the old one.
  • Much She is much smarter than people think.
  • A lot The new phone is a lot more expensive.
  • A great deal This exam was a great deal harder than the last one.
  • Significantly Profits are significantly higher this quarter.
  • Twice He is twice older than me.
  • Easily She is easily more qualified for the position.
  • By a long way It's more difficult by a long way.

Intensifiers for LESS

a bit, a little, slightly.

  • A bit I'm feeling a bit more tired today.
  • A little Can you speak a little louder?
  • Slightly The temperature is slightly lower now.

Structure

as + adj + as / the same + noun + as
  • as + adj + as She is as intelligent as her sister.
  • the same + noun + as They have the same car as we do.

Intensifiers for Equality

nearly, nowhere near, almost, just.

  • Nearly He is nearly as tall as his brother.
  • Nowhere near His car is nowhere near as fast as mine.
  • Almost The meal was almost as good as homemade.
  • Just She is just as talented as her rival.

Structure

the + -est / the most + adj

Intensifiers for Superlatives

easily, by far, by a long way.

  • Easily He is easily the best player on the team.
  • By far She is by far the most talented artist here.
  • By a long way This is the worst movie by a long way.

Double Comparative

the + more / -er / less, the + more / -er / less
  • More / More The more I study, the more I learn.
  • -er / -er The harder you work, the luckier you get.
  • Less / Less The less I sleep, the less energy I have.
  • Mixed The older we grow, the more we know.

Speaking Strategy (C1/C2)

Estructuras comodín para hacer comparaciones avanzadas en el examen.

Dónde usarlo: Part 3 o Part 4. Casi siempre sale el tema del estrés, las pantallas o la vida frenética. Úsalo ahí para mostrar causa/efecto.

The more [A], the more/less [B]
  • Example The more time people spend interacting through screens, the more isolated they seem to become.
  • Traducción Cuanto más tiempo pasa la gente interactuando a través de pantallas, más aislados parecen volverse.

Dónde usarlo: Part 2 (Long Turn). Perfecto para comparar dos fotos muy opuestas (ej. naturaleza vs ciudad, teoría vs práctica, ocio activo vs pasivo).

[Option A] is nowhere near as [adj] as [Option B]
  • Example Reading about this online is nowhere near as effective as actually experiencing it firsthand.
  • Traducción Leer sobre esto por internet no es ni de lejos tan efectivo como experimentarlo de primera mano.

Dónde usarlo: Part 3 (Decision making). Justo en el último minuto, cuando el examinador os interrumpe y os pide elegir la mejor opción de todas.

Taking everything into consideration, [Option] is by far the most...
  • Example Taking everything into consideration, I'd say this option is by far the most practical one.
  • Traducción Teniendo todo en cuenta, diría que esta opción es con diferencia la más práctica.

Gerund vs To-Infinitive

Grammar Theory

Speaking Strategy (C1/C2)

Estructuras comodín para usar Gerundios e Infinitivos en el examen Speaking.

Dónde usarlo: Part 4 (Discussion). Cuando te pidan tu opinión personal sobre un tema reflexivo profundo (relaciones humanas, trabajo, prioridades).

I can't help feeling that... [opinión]
  • Example I can't help feeling that people are becoming increasingly disconnected from their surroundings.
  • Traducción No puedo evitar sentir que la gente se está desconectando cada vez más de su entorno.

Dónde usarlo: Part 3 (Collaborative Task). Cuando estés debatiendo el diagrama y quieras descartar elegantemente una opción para pasar a la siguiente.

It's simply not worth [gerund]...
  • Example It's simply not worth spending time on options that won't make a real difference, so we ought to focus on...
  • Traducción Simplemente no vale la pena dedicar tiempo a opciones que no marcarán una diferencia real, así que deberíamos centrarnos en...

Dónde usarlo: Part 4 (Discussion). Al hablar sobre la dificultad del ser humano para cambiar (dieta, ecología, adicción al móvil).

Despite [gerund]..., many people still struggle to [infinitive]...
  • Example Despite being fully aware of the consequences, the vast majority of people still struggle to change their daily habits.
  • Traducción A pesar de ser plenamente conscientes de las consecuencias, a la gran mayoría de personas todavía le cuesta cambiar sus hábitos diarios.
